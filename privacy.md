# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

QR Scanner & Generator ("we", "our", or "us") operates the QR Scanner & Generator mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We design our App with privacy at the forefront. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers.

### A. Camera & Image Processing
* **Real-Time Scanning:** The App requires access to your device's camera solely to scan barcodes and QR codes in real time. All camera frames and image analysis are processed **locally on your device** using Google ML Kit. Camera footage is never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any cloud server or third party.
* **Gallery Scanning:** When you choose to scan a QR code from your device's photo gallery using the Android Photo Picker, the selected image is analyzed locally on-device and is never transmitted or stored externally.

### B. Local Scan History
* **SQLite Database:** Any scan results you choose to save are stored strictly in a local database (`Room`) on your device. This data remains exclusively on your device and is never synchronized to an external cloud database. You have full control to delete individual history records or clear all history at any time.

### C. Advertisements (Google AdMob)
* The App integrates Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and app open advertisements to support free usage of the app. Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and advertising IDs in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. 
* Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" subscription will have all advertisements permanently disabled.

### D. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)
* All subscription purchases ("Remove Ads") are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or financial information. All billing data is handled securely by Google LLC in accordance with Google Play's Terms of Service.

## 2. Permissions Required
The App requests the following permissions for core functionality:
* **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Required to preview the camera feed and scan QR codes / barcodes.
* **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Required by Google AdMob to fetch advertisements and by Google Play Billing to verify active subscriptions.

## 3. Data Security
Because all scan data and camera processing remain locally on your device, your information is protected by your device's built-in operating system security measures.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. 

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
